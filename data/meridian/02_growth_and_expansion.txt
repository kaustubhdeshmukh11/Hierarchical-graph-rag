MERIDIAN HEALTH SYSTEMS — GROWTH AND EXPANSION (2017-2019)

In April 2017, Meridian Health Systems raised $18 million in Series B funding led by Tiger Global Management, with participation from existing investors Sequoia Capital India and HealthVenture Partners. The round valued the company at $85 million. The funding was earmarked for three priorities: expanding MediScan's AI capabilities, entering the pharmaceutical supply chain market, and geographic expansion to Southeast Asia.

Following the funding, Dr. Ananya Kapoor hired Sameer Joshi, a veteran supply chain executive who had spent 15 years at Cipla Ltd, as VP of Pharma Solutions. Joshi was tasked with building PharmaTrack, a blockchain-based pharmaceutical supply chain tracking system designed to combat counterfeit medications in India. The World Health Organization had estimated that up to 20% of medications sold in India were counterfeit or substandard, making this a critical healthcare issue.

PharmaTrack launched in beta in October 2017 with three pharmaceutical distributors: MedSupply India, PharmaCorp Global, and HealthLine Distributors. The system used distributed ledger technology to create an immutable record of every medication from manufacturing to patient delivery. Each drug package received a unique QR code that could be scanned at every point in the supply chain, and patients could verify authenticity using a free mobile app.

Meanwhile, Dr. Vikram Nair expanded MediScan's capabilities significantly. In June 2018, MediScan v2.0 was released with support for detecting diabetic retinopathy from retinal scans, achieving 96.1% accuracy in clinical validation at Sankara Eye Hospital in Bangalore. The model was developed in collaboration with Dr. Aisha Rahman, an ophthalmology AI researcher whom Meridian hired from Google Health in January 2018.

The NovaCare Clinics partnership deepened in 2018 when Dr. Rajesh Sundaram agreed to deploy MediScan in all 120 NovaCare centers, expanding from the initial 35 pilot sites. This $4.5 million deal also included real-time HealthBridge integration, creating a seamless workflow where patient imaging data flowed directly into MediScan for analysis and results were automatically recorded in the patient's HealthBridge profile.

In March 2019, Meridian entered the Southeast Asian market by acquiring TeleMed Singapore, a small telehealth startup with 15 employees and existing partnerships with 20 clinics in Singapore and Malaysia. The acquisition cost $3.2 million and was led by Priya Sharma, who relocated to Singapore to establish Meridian's APAC headquarters. TeleMed's existing video consultation platform was integrated with HealthBridge, creating Meridian's first cross-border health data exchange capability.

By mid-2019, Meridian had 210 employees across four offices (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Singapore, and Kuala Lumpur), processed over 25 million patient records annually, and had revenue of $12 million. PharmaTrack had tracked over 50 million medication packages, with a counterfeit detection rate of 3.4% — meaning it flagged approximately 1.7 million potentially counterfeit drugs that would have otherwise reached patients.
